**What is stored about you**
- In the group's app (the bot passes your presses and commands on to it): your Discord name and roles, your account, your Steam ID and squad.xml nick if you link with `/link`, your operation sign-ups (slots, maybe, can't come, reserve), your attendance from the server (who played, and for how long), and the mission ratings you give, with your comment.
- In the bot: the Steam link reminders it sent you, and your messages in operation channels and their threads once a channel is moved into its archive (after about a year).

**What is published**
- squad.xml: your Steam ID, nick, Discord username and remarks. The file is public, at the address `/link` gives you.

**How long**
- Without limit: operations and attendance serve the group's statistics.
- Database backups expire after a couple of weeks.

**Erasing your data**
- Ask staff. Your account is deleted, you become a pseudonym (former:<n>) in sign-ups, attendance and ratings, your Steam link goes, and your archived messages are emptied. Past operations still add up, but no longer point to you.

**What the bot stores about you**
- Your Steam ID and squad.xml nick, if you link your account with `/link`.
- Your event sign-ups (slots, maybe, can't come, reserve) and your attendance from the server (who played, and for how long).
- The mission ratings you give, with your comment.
- Messages in event channels and their threads, once a channel is moved into the database archive (after about a year).

**What the bot publishes**
- squad.xml: your Steam ID, nick, Discord username and specializations. The file is public, at the address `/link` gives you.

**How long**
- Without limit: event data and attendance serve the group's statistics.
- Your Steam link is removed by itself when you lose your rank for longer (90 days by default).
- Database backups expire after a couple of weeks.

**Erasing your data**
- Ask the bot administrators. Your Steam ID and Discord account are replaced by a pseudonym everywhere, your Steam link is deleted, and your archived messages are emptied. Past events still add up, but no longer point to you.
